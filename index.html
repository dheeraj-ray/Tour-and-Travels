Visa requirements vary by destination and your nationality. Most of our packages include visa assistance and guidance. When you book a trip, our team will provide a detailed visa checklist specific to your destination and help you through the entire application process. We recommend applying at least 4-6 weeks before your travel date.

We offer flexible cancellation policies. Cancellations made 30+ days before departure receive a full refund minus a small processing fee. 15-29 days before departure: 50% refund. Less than 15 days: no refund but you can reschedule. We strongly recommend purchasing travel insurance for added protection.

Travel insurance is not included by default but is highly recommended.
